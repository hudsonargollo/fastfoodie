Currency & exchange rate

Why one price is in Bs and the rest are in US dollars

The split, explained

Software prices, monthly fees and the marketing add-on are quoted and settled in US dollars. The Plan 2 per-order fee is quoted and settled in Bolivianos. No other line item is denominated in Bs.

Because the two Plan 2 options sit in different currencies, the break-even point moves with the exchange rate. At the Bs 11.50 convention used throughout, the two cost the same at 1,725 orders per month — below that, paying per order is the cheaper structure. Try it on your own volume.

  • Working rate1 USD = Bs 11.50
  • Bs 1 per order equals≈ $0.087
  • $150 per month equalsBs 1,725

Bs 11.50 is the single convention used for every conversion on this page and in the calculator, so the two fee structures compare on the same basis. It does not bind either party. Bolivia has historically operated both an official central bank rate and a prevailing market rate, and they can differ substantially — the rate source and review frequency are named in the signed contract.

Questions

Before you choose a plan

Do I get the same software on both plans?

Yes. Both plans include the full delivery automation deployment, order intake and processing automation, and onboarding with initial configuration. The difference is the payment structure, not the feature set.

What happens when the included licence year ends on Plan 1?

Renewal terms after the included licence period are set out in your service contract, so there is no surprise change to your cost or access. Ask us and we will confirm them in writing before you sign.

Can I change between the fixed monthly fee and the per-order fee?

Your fee structure is chosen at contract signing and applies for the term of the agreement unless amended in writing. Review your average monthly order volume first — the currency note above shows where the two cross over.

Is the marketing add-on required?

No. It is optional and layered on top of either plan. If you take it while on the Full Package, the rate drops from $250 to $150 per month, or to $99 per month with the 24-month prepaid term.

Are the marketing campaigns connected to my customer list?

That is the point of the add-on. Alongside new lead generation, it runs re-engagement campaigns against the customer base you already have, so existing contacts convert into repeat delivery orders.

What is not included in these prices?

Applicable sales, VAT or withholding taxes are excluded and are the client’s responsibility where legally required. Work outside the scope described in the proposal is quoted separately as a change order.

How do I get started?

Pick a plan and send it to us by email or WhatsApp. We confirm the scope and fee structure, issue the service contract, and schedule onboarding and configuration before your go-live date.
