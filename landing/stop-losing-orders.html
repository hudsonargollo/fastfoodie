Seven things that cost you orders every single day

None of these are a staffing problem. They are what happens when orders arrive on four different channels and a person has to move them by hand.

Four channels, one pair of hands

WhatsApp, phone, walk-in and delivery apps all land somewhere different. Somebody reads each one and types it again.

Cost: staff time you already pay for, spent copying

Rush hour is when orders drop

The busy hour is exactly when a message gets buried, an address is misread, or the same order is taken twice.

Cost: refunds, remakes, a customer who never comes back

“Where is my order?” calls

Cost: the counter stops serving the person in front of it

Nothing is ready when the rider is

Fair questions

What owners ask before they reply

We already take orders on WhatsApp. Why change?

WhatsApp is fine for the customer and hard for the kitchen. The problem is not receiving the order — it is that a person then has to move it into preparation, into a rider’s hands and into a record. Automation keeps WhatsApp as the front door and removes the copying behind it.

Is this going to slow my staff down while they learn it?

Setup and initial configuration are done with you during onboarding, before go-live, so your team meets a system that already matches how your kitchen works.

Training is one working session, and your staff are taking orders on it the same day. There is no learning curve to ride out in the middle of service.

Do I need to buy hardware?

No. It runs on the phones and computers you already own, connected to the order channels you already use. There is no terminal to buy and nothing new to install at the counter.

We will state this plainly in your quote so there is no surprise cost after signing.

How long until it is running?

Usually within a week of signing. The work is configuration, not development: your menu, your channels, your staff logins. The date is agreed before you sign, not after.

The full year of licence included with the Full Package starts on your go-live date, not on the day you sign.

What does it cost, and is there a contract?

Two ways to buy: pay once for a full year of licence with no monthly fee, or start with a lower upfront amount and pay monthly. Rights and obligations sit in the signed service contract, and you see the terms before you commit.
