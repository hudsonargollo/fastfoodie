The pain The fix
Pain 01

A tax on your regulars

The app found the customer once, then takes a cut of every repeat order. Your most loyal buyers become your most expensive ones.

Fix 01

Your own repeat-order channel

We move recurring customers to a channel you own, priced at Bs 1 per order instead of a percentage of the ticket.

Pain 02

You don't own the customer

Orders through the app leave the name and number with the platform. You can't call a regular back, and you can't build a base.

Fix 02

A customer base that stays yours

Every order builds your own list, ready for retention and re-activation — the asset the app keeps for itself.

Pain 03

Manual entry loses orders

Staff re-type orders from WhatsApp, phone and apps during the rush. Mistakes, delays and missed tickets cost real revenue.

Fix 03

Orders captured automatically

Intake flows straight to the kitchen with no re-typing, so peak hour stops leaking orders.

Pain 04

No view of what's working

Without channel-level numbers you can't see what sells, which margins survive, or where to spend next.

Fix 04

Consultancy-led business intelligence

We audit how you sell online, design the strategic plan and report on it — decisions on numbers, not guesswork.

Pain 05

Tech that takes months to land

Long implementations, new hardware and a learning curve stall the kitchen in the middle of service.

Fix 05

Live in a week, trained in a session

Configuration, not development: your menu, channels and logins. Staff take orders the same day; no hardware to buy.

Pain 06

Marketing without a plan

Scattered posts and boosted ads spend budget without a funnel or a measurable return.

Fix 06

A designed, executed growth plan

The consultancy builds the funnel; the optional add-on runs search and social against it, with reporting.
